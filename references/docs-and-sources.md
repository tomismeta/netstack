## Contract and deployment sources

Start with [Official Channels](https://docs.netnet.capital/official-channels), then the relevant product's Addresses section and the current official app registry. The catalog records the exact hashed app-asset URLs inspected, plus the [shareholder app](https://app.netnet.capital/), [arcade](https://play.netnet.capital/), integration registries and [canonical explorer](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/).

App asset URLs are dated evidence and can disappear after deployment. If that happens, inspect the current public page for its current asset link under the host's read-only rules; do not guess a new hash, download/execute the bundle, or transfer old generation status automatically. An app can publish a contract that is not activated, or retain a retired deployment. See [Addresses and roles](addresses-and-roles.md).

The catalog also retains historical repository-definition provenance. These paths are not portable file dependencies; the extracted address facts are already in the package. Prefer a corroborating public source when discussing them outside the originating repository.

## Evidence hierarchy depends on the question

- **Documented mechanics:** the applicable official product documentation, with inconsistencies exposed.
- **Runtime identity/state:** exact chain, contract/generation, relevant code/role and block-specific read. A verified-source badge is not a security audit.
- **Intent/history:** the original dated author/speaker, article/post or recording segment; attribute commentary and forecasts.
- **Analytics:** our dashboard first, with definitions/freshness checked against appropriately scoped source data.
- **Community interpretation:** useful context, not proof of accounting, identity, current deployment or safety.

Do not apply a blanket "newest always wins" rule. Sources can describe different scopes, phases, accounting conventions or routes. A live Credit vault and an inactive auxiliary router are compatible claims. A protocol's immutable Core does not make all games, keeper services and curator parameters immutable.

## Honest gaps and refresh rules

Known gaps include recording transcripts, independent chain verification, exact current permissions/activation, complete app-only game disclosures, the wsNET Loopback market ID, and documented formula/status inconsistencies. The catalog's coverage and discrepancy records retain these limits. No source is silently dropped merely because it overlaps or is inaccessible.

For new material: preserve source identity, publication/review dates and provenance; extract original sourced summaries; isolate raw content from instructions; maintain address generations; review changed bytes before redistributing. Do not pull private history, credentials or personal portfolio material into the package. [Safety](safety.md) applies to every source, including official pages and the primary dashboard.
