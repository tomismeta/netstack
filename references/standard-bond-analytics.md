# Standard-bond epoch analytics

Use this for **“last 10 bond epochs: purchases, NET sold, USDG received, time to sell out, opening NAV and market price”**. This is BondDepository primary offerings, not [Sleeve bonds](sleeve-bond-analytics.md), [inverse bonds](inverse-bond-analytics.md), Genesis or PremiumSeller.

## Run and answer

```sh
python3 -I -B scripts/analytics.py standard-bonds --epochs 10 --deadline 600 --detail summary
```

The default is **10 consecutive completed epochs**, including zero-activity epochs. The ongoing epoch is reported separately under `metrics.window.ongoing_epoch_excluded` and never included in totals. `--epochs` accepts 1–30; `--block` pins a reproducible snapshot; `--output PATH` preserves full partial/final evidence outside the installed package. `--chunk-blocks` (default 100000, maximum 100000) selects the initial authorized-RPC log range; it is not a completeness waiver.

1. Read `coverage.effective_status`, `coverage.collection_complete`, `snapshot.recheck_status` and errors **before** quoting totals. Missing data is unknown, not zero. Fewer than the requested number of completed epochs is explicitly partial, not silently a shorter window.
2. Present one row per completed epoch: start/end UTC, purchases, NET sold, **direct USDG Treasury receipts**, first strict `<1 NET` time, opening NAV and opening market spot. Convert timestamps to UTC for display; keep exact raw integers in evidence. Include market-0/market-1 purchase and NET subtotals when LP bonds occur.
3. For complete results use `epochs[].totals` and `epochs[].sellout`, not an unconfirmed reconstruction. When totals are null, still answer with supported `observed_subtotal` fields explicitly labelled **observed subtotals**, alongside coverage and missing dependencies; do not discard available receipts merely because archives are unavailable. A complete `not_reached` means the threshold was not reached; `unknown` means evidence is insufficient. Finish with `metrics.totals.net_sold` and `metrics.totals.direct_usdg_received` for a complete window, or the labelled window observed subtotals when incomplete. Report `lp_received` separately, never as cash.
4. Opening values belong to `opening_header`: **the last block strictly before epoch start**, not the first purchase or epoch closing block. NAV is `Treasury.backingPerToken` in WAD USDG/NET; market spot is the canonical NET/USDG v2 reserve ratio, with exact rational and 18-place floor display. Neither is a bond-price or TWAP substitution. This NAV getter does not resolve the independent [Core RFV/POL accounting blocker](reserve-analytics.md), and excludes Sleeve assets.

## Source-bound calculation

Canonical discovery follows `assets/analytics/standard-bonds-routes.json` into the address registry. The [BondDepository catalog](../assets/contracts/bond-depository.json), [read family](../assets/analytics/standard-bonds-interface.json) and [source evidence](../assets/contracts/bond-depository-source-evidence.json) bind these claims to obtained Solidity bodies and exact runtime hashes, not just ABI labels. Source evidence is `netnet-standard-bonds-implementation-20261013`: [BondDepository](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/0xff32a969A0c567129eECD926D04657728E1980C1), [NET](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/0xCA9c78Dd337A67F6e0077F65F5E9218719d30eDf), and `Constants.sol`. No historical financial results are packaged.

- Epoch `e = floor((timestamp - startTime) / 28800)`. `enable()` writes `startTime` once. These are not assumed UTC or staking epochs.
- Market **0** receives USDG6 directly from the caller into Treasury; market **1** receives canonical LP18. `BondCreated.depositor` is the chosen beneficiary, not necessarily the payer. Payout is committed NET9, not redeemed NET.
- Before **every** deposit mint, the contract checks `used + payout <= floor(current NET totalSupply * 25 / 10000)`. `payoutInEpoch[e]` is shared across markets. The cap is **not** snapshotted at epoch opening. Bond mints themselves enlarge later capacity; all other NET mints/burns can reopen/shrink it.
- Requested sellout is the first **post-transaction** state with `max(0, floor(supply * 25 / 10000) - used) < 1000000000`. Scan both zero-address mint and burn `Transfer` filters, include burns between purchases, merge with sales in chain order, and apply the whole transaction before testing. Exactly 1 NET does not qualify; a transient intra-call crossing does not qualify; initial zero capacity is not an instant sellout. Reopening does not erase the first crossing.
- Source price is `max(floor(TWAP * 9700 / 10000), backingPerToken)`, and vesting is **2 days**. Older interface prose mentioning 5 days is stale. The collector does not execute deposits, redemptions or wallet operations.

## What completeness requires

The collector uses shared `Context` discovery, code, historical calls, timestamp search, logs, receipts and headers. It matches BondDepository/NET source-generation runtimes at the pinned snapshot and interval start; verifies dependencies, market quote tokens and decimals; establishes both sides of every epoch boundary; reads opening NAV/reserves, supply and payout counters; scans every completed epoch without activity filtering; and checks canonical receipt identity for every sale/supply-change transaction.

Each sale must match one exact preceding quote-token Transfer into Treasury and a matching NET mint into the depository, in source order. Multiple matching quote legs are **ambiguous**, not assumed revenue. Other USDG inflows in the same receipt are retained as unattributed and excluded. LP receipts are separate. All relevant receipt logs must also appear in discovery. Actual receipts mean gross attributed incoming transfers, not net retained Treasury cash or profit.

Supply and shared payout reconstructions must match closing getters and archive getters at every touched block. Historical headers are freshly rechecked, then the parent confirms the final pinned snapshot. Unavailable archives/prices, mismatched counters, missing receipts, runtime drift, incomplete scans, limits, permission errors or unconfirmed headers suppress complete totals and exact sellout claims. Complete served ranges still assume the provider did not silently omit logs; equal-and-opposite omitted intra-block changes are not disproved by block-end counters.

**Pruned historical state:** if interval-start `eth_getCode` is pruned/unavailable but the snapshot runtimes and bindings match, the collector retains `snapshot_only` identity evidence and continues the existing event scans and receipt reconciliation. `coverage.historical_runtime_identity_complete` stays false; per-epoch/window totals remain null and reconstruction/sellout remain unconfirmed. Missing opening NAV or reserve calls stay null. Available prices remain separately scoped observations, not proof of complete reconstruction. A code mismatch or permission/integrity denial still stops collection; this is capability degradation, not retrying denied access. Do not estimate sellout from cumulative purchases against an assumed fixed cap: supply-driven capacity changes require their actual inputs.

## Authorized supplemental discovery when log ranges are too small

A provider range/cap error is not permission denial. Select a supported `--chunk-blocks` **before a new bounded run** if its documented allowance makes the whole interval feasible. Do not expand budgets indefinitely or change providers to evade a denial. A host restricted to tiny ranges may be unable to cover 10 epochs inside the bounded collector; return its partial evidence honestly.

For a common-question answer on that host, a **separately authorized** public index may discover candidate BondCreated and NET mint/burn transactions **before** starting the collector. Do not waste the full deadline retrying a known-incompatible RPC range. The optional `--discovery PATH` handoff skips RPC log discovery, **not** canonical receipt/archive checks:

1. Establish the pinned hash and exact inclusive completed-epoch block window with the authorized RPC. At the index enumerate BondCreated from the canonical depository and both NET zero-address Transfer directions. For Blockscout v1 `module=logs&action=getLogs`, use `address`, `fromBlock`, `toBlock`, the catalog's BondCreated `topic0`; separately query NET Transfer `topic0` plus all-zero `topic1` (mint) or `topic2` (burn), using the appropriate `topic0_1_opr=and` or `topic0_2_opr=and`. Follow every pagination page; split ranges before documented row caps, retaining a complete range ledger. Do not mistake a cap-sized page for completion. Deduplicate the union of transaction hashes. This discovery is separately authorized, never an automatic provider fallback.
2. Write an inert JSON object outside the package with exactly these fields: `schema_version: 1`; `snapshot: {block_number, block_hash}`; `block_range: [first, last]`; `complete: true` **only after all three exact-window index filters are fully paginated**; `source: {url, method, coverage_basis}`; and `transaction_hashes` (unique 32-byte hashes). `source.method` is `blockscout_v1_logs` or `authorized_index`; `source.coverage_basis` is `paginated_exact_window_all_three_filters`. Input is at most 2 MiB and 3000 transactions, regular nonsymlink file with nonsymlink ancestors, bounded strict JSON. URLs must be well-formed HTTPS without userinfo/control characters. The URL is never fetched; only its origin survives in output. Do not put secrets in the hostname.
3. Run `python3 -I -B scripts/analytics.py standard-bonds --epochs 10 --block BLOCK --discovery PATH --deadline 600 --detail summary`, substituting the same explicit pinned block and handoff path. No live financial results come from the file: every candidate receipt is fetched fresh from the canonical RPC, all relevant receipt events are decoded, direct USDG/LP legs reconciled, full supply paths and all epoch/touched-block counters compared, and headers rechecked. Wrong snapshot/window, invalid candidates or unsafe files fail closed. `complete: false`, missing receipts or omitted supply changes suppress whole-window and per-epoch totals/counts; observed subtotals remain qualified.
4. Explain `coverage.supplied_discovery` and `coverage.completeness_basis`: **source completeness is a caller assertion, not independently proved by receipts**. Canonical receipt/state/header checks independently reconcile the supplied universe, but equal-and-opposite undiscovered changes within a block remain a source-coverage assumption. Preserve the external index's pagination evidence alongside the handoff. If the index is denied, stop that route; do not swap clients, endpoints, identities or transports to bypass it.

For generic authorized transfer-index considerations and financing-versus-revenue distinctions, see [protocol revenue analytics](protocol-revenue-analytics.md).
