# Fresh protocol revenue and proceeds by product / asset

Use this umbrella for “overall protocol revenue”, “what earns money for NET?”, or a breakdown by product, asset or destination. Investigate the whole discovered ecosystem, including NetLong, rather than returning only Core tax or today's holdings. **There is no bundled whole-protocol `revenue` total.** The bounded `netlong-revenue` command and other collectors supply evidence for specific rows; authorized supplemental read-only research supplies the rest. No packaged transaction history, balances or financial snapshots are inputs.

**Evidence date:** October 12, 2026 consolidation; independent fresh reads covered the [current documentation index](https://docs.netnet.capital/llms.txt), [Official Channels](https://docs.netnet.capital/official-channels), [Treasury](https://docs.netnet.capital/treasury), [fee schedule](https://docs.netnet.capital/FEES.HTM), [Credit](https://docs.netnet.capital/credit), [RWA Desk](https://docs.netnet.capital/rwa-desk), [TURBO](https://docs.netnet.capital/turbo), [COINflip](https://docs.netnet.capital/coinflip), [Rialto registry](https://docs.rialto.xyz/developers/router-registries.md), and [TaxCollector explorer source/ABI](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/0x086C58400b8708Ef993f256E12e752dcF0AC918e). Other linked product mechanics retain the dated evidence and limitations in their topic references; this is not a fresh audit of every deployment or any live financial amount.

## 1. Answer the question without hiding the accounting choice

If context specifies a period, use it, including the stated timezone for “today” or “this week”. For unqualified cumulative wording such as “overall revenue” or “how much has it generated?”, follow the [PremiumSeller convention](premium-seller-analytics.md): **lifetime through a fresh pinned endpoint**, with deployment/generation-specific starts. State that choice and proceed without a blocking questionnaire. Do not silently substitute24hours or the LP helper's seven-day window. Pin opening `A` and closing `B` by block timestamps and report requested and actually covered `(A,B]` times; deployment-to-B scans include the creation block and establish pre-creation zero state only where justified. If lifetime history is inaccessible, preserve useful observed-window subtotals and continue authorized research, with the requested lifetime total unresolved. A series spanning an explicit period boundary needs opening liabilities/receivables or a separately labelled completed-cycle result, not its entire lifetime fees assigned to its closing day.

Present **two parallel, non-additive views**:

1. **Earned economics:** external fees, attributable lending yield, realized inventory gains and underwriting results, separated by basis. Show gross product charges, protocol-beneficial portion, Manager-personal / third-party portion, internal eliminations, and attributable costs/losses. These are not all independently additive: an underwriting result can already include its fees; lending yield can already be net of fees. State which components the subtotal includes.
2. **Treasury / Sleeve receipts and capital deployment:** actual received assets, retained inventory, pending claims, financing and disposal proceeds, principal recovery, internal remittances, expenses and buybacks. Include PremiumSeller, bonds and subscriptions here even though they are not ordinary earned fee revenue. Reconcile cash without relabelling every inflow income.

Default beneficiary columns distinguish **Core Treasury**, **Manager-custodied protocol-benefit Sleeve**, **other evidenced protocol-beneficial interests**, **Manager's own business**, **outside capital**, and **unattributed**. The broad economic activity of a branded product is useful context, not automatic protocol ownership. The Sleeve's stated benefit is a publisher attribution, not a Core guarantee or NET-holder legal redemption right. A Manager-owned casino bankroll is not silently included in protocol equity. For a user requesting management-company consolidation, explicitly change the perimeter and eliminations.

| Basis | Meaning / required distinction |
|---|---|
| Earned / assessed fee | Applicable external charge in its native asset, net of reversals/refunds as appropriate; separate assessment from unconditional entitlement. |
| Accrued entitlement | Unpaid interest, fee shares or fee bucket supported by applicable accrual and ownership. Endpoint pending amount is a stock, not period earnings. |
| Cash-realized receipt | Actual asset received, with receipt/custody evidence. Shares, marks and internal credits are not cash. In-kind assets can be realized **in kind**, without being USDG cash. |
| Realized gain / loss | Disposal or completed outcome consideration less attributable cost basis and relevant costs; gross proceeds alone are not gain. |
| Distribution / allocation | Transfer of an existing earned amount to an owner, router, reserve or burn path; not another earning event. |
| Net profit / loss | Compatible earned results less all required costs/losses, including financing, underwriting, bad debt, inventory cost, prizes, incentives, refunds and operating costs. Missing material costs means profit unavailable, not gross fees relabelled profit. |
| Financing / principal / supply | Deposits, borrowing, capital issuance, principal return, own-token mint/burn, staking rebase, wrapping and internal transfers. Report material proceeds/outflows, exclude from earned revenue. |

### Core tax: independently obtained source and exact events

October12 [TaxCollector source/ABI](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/0x086C58400b8708Ef993f256E12e752dcF0AC918e) returned explorer-verified Solidity, dependencies and runtime. This is **un-pinned explorer evidence**, not independently compiled equivalence or live execution. The source establishes these concrete reads: `net()`, `usdg()`, `router()`, `pairContract()`, `oracle()`, `treasury()`, `teamWallet()`, `pTeam()`, `teamBps()`, `treasuryBps()`, `pendingNet()`, `wired()`. Cross-check addresses and token decimals at both boundaries. `pendingNet()` is simply NET `balanceOf(collector)`: arbitrary transfers can contaminate a presumed tax bucket.

`Converted(uint256,uint256,uint256,uint256)` has **no indexed fields**; four data words are `netIn, usdgOutRaw, teamUsdgRaw, treasuryUsdgRaw`. Hash with Ethereum Keccak-256, not SHA3-256. The obtained source swaps bounded NET, measures only the new USDG balance delta, then uses `teamShare=floor(usdgOut*teamBps/500)` and `treasuryShare=usdgOut-teamShare`. That500 is the total tax basis-point count, **not10000** for this allocation. `teamBps` starts at400 in `Constants` and decays from the pTEAM clock; stale interface comments saying300 do not override executable source. ## 5. Consolidate once, in native units

Use one row per product/generation/asset/basis/beneficiary, with this minimum schema:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity | `product`, `generation`, chain ID, contract address, asset chain/address/decimals; parent market/series/NFT when applicable. |
| Period and basis | Requested/covered timestamps, A/B hashes, earning/settlement time, basis above, gross versus net, included costs and ownership fraction effective at that event. |
| Amount | Exact signed raw amount or rational and native units; `null` for unknown. Opening/closing pending, refunds/losses and actual receipts as distinct linked rows. |
| Beneficiary / destination | Economic owner/category and evidence; immediate receiver, ultimate destination, custody versus beneficiary, realized asset retained or converted. |
| Consolidation | `economic_event_id`, linked observations, external payer versus internal origin, duplicate-of/elimination, financing/principal classification, whether a net result already includes this fee. |
| Coverage / provenance | Complete selected scope, bounded observed subtotal, conditional bound or unknown; discovered/uninspected products/generations/IDs/ranges; public source URLs/dates, runtime/source qualification, getter block/result or transaction/log references, residuals and missing inputs. |
| Optional valuation | Dated price source/block/round, exact quote units/scales, methodology (transaction-time or explicitly common mark), freshness and resulting currency; keep native quantities alongside. |

**Elimination rules:**

- An external trader's separately assessed trading fee, NET levy and AMM charge can each be genuine distinct charges. Establish the **economic payer** and fee bases; do not deduplicate merely because they occur in one receipt.
- A product fee subsequently sent to a router, swapped for NET, taxed internally, burned or remitted to Treasury is not fresh external revenue at every stop. In the consolidated perimeter eliminate internal tax/fee portions and matching internal expense; outside-owned LP/execution costs remain external expenses. Product-origin revenue and destination receipts are two dimensions of the same flow, not additive tables.
- Interest or charges paid by the Sleeve to its own fee beneficiary eliminate, but portions owed to independent lenders/LPs do not. A related-party fee visible on chain can be real at entity level yet zero incremental consolidated revenue.
- Eliminate a vault claim against its underlying, treasury remittance against the originating fee, and house distribution against included house earnings. Leave Manager-personal and outside shareholder amounts outside default protocol-owned totals, even if the same Safe operates the product.
- Burning reduces supply; minting, staking index growth, RFV/NAV/share-price increase, reduced float and a rising stock mark are not external revenue or cash profit. Acquiring NET inventory is not burning it. Buying and retiring own NET below NAV is not earning the spread in cash.

Sum only compatible native assets and accounting bases. USDG is not USD: converting `u` USDG to USD requires a dated acceptable USDG/USD price; converting a stock's USD mark to USDG requires division by that price. Historical receipts valued at today's mark are a **common-mark comparison**, not the cash received or historical realized result. Unpriced assets remain native rows; do not drop them or silently use parity. wsNET needs its applicable index and price at the stated valuation time, not today's index applied to old receipts.

A capital-aware matched-period bridge is available **only** with complete compatible net assets, liabilities, ownership changes, external contributions/distributions, losses and valuation inputs:

```text
comprehensive economic result = net_assets_B - net_assets_A
                              - external capital contributions
                              + external capital distributions
```

This result can include unrealized marks; isolate them before calling a residual realized profit. Borrowing raises assets and liabilities together; principal repayment reduces both. Primary issuance/own-token repurchases belong in the capital bridge under the declared perimeter, not operating earnings. In-kind capital and inventory consideration need explicit dated values. Insurance shortfalls, bad debt, unpaid prizes/player claims, accrued borrowing costs, donations and unclassified transfers must be resolved rather than plugged into profit. A complete cash ledger alone does not prove complete net assets.

## 6. Report useful partial results honestly

Lead with the period, beneficiary perimeter and supported earned/receipt breakdown, then the material missing rows. **Observed zero** requires applicable semantics and complete evidence for that metric/window; **unknown** means missing evidence, not zero; **bounded subtotal** names its searched subset. A mathematical lower bound is appropriate only when omitted contributions cannot be negative and ownership/valuation bounds support it. Omitted costs or losses generally invalidate a lower-bound claim for profit.

Keep lifetime flows separate from point-in-time **inventory, outstanding claim and available capacity**: an empty current bucket can follow lifetime receipts, a positive claim need not be paid, and capacity is not historical proceeds. Likewise, zero remittance from the scoped NetLong routers does not establish zero Treasury revenue from tax conversion, other products, indirect paths or earlier generations.

Full protocol totals require complete relevant discovery, generation/asset/beneficiary attribution, interval coverage, source applicability, reconciled flows and compatible valuations. A complete helper invocation or a reconciled single product does not establish that. Keep the full requested total unavailable when any required input is missing; provide exact supported native subtotals and the next source/read needed for each gap. No extrapolated daily fee estimate presented as observed revenue, no lifetime claim from a current bucket, and no broad refusal that discards established evidence.
