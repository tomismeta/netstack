# PremiumSeller historical accounting

Use the optional standard-library collector for questions such as “how much NET has PremiumSeller sold?”, “at what realized prices?”, and “how much USDG did it deliver to Treasury?” It retrieves fresh logs and receipts on each invocation; the package contains no cached financial history.

```sh
python3 -I -B scripts/analytics.py premiumseller --detail summary
python3 -I -B scripts/analytics.py premiumseller --since-days 30 --block latest-2 --detail full --deadline 600
python3 -I -B scripts/analytics.py premiumseller --block 12345678 --output /tmp/premiumseller-run.json
```

The Robinhood chain-4663 HTTPS RPC is selected before the run: `NETSTACK_RPC_URL` may specify a host-authorized endpoint; otherwise the public RPC is used. The explicit read-only ABI allowlist, pinned block, deadline, response/log limits, receipt identity checks, block recheck, and safe output handling are the same as the other analytics commands; see [transport configuration](integrations.md). No wallet, signing, installation, provider switching during collection, `execute` call, write RPC, or transaction artifact is involved. Permission denial is terminal; do not retry another provider or route around it. Summary only projects stdout; `--output` preserves full evidence checkpoints. Exit 0 means the requested collection completed; 2 means partial/blocked; 1 means invalid arguments or fatal failure.

## Identity, history and bounded coverage

The canonical seller is `0x346e1a31171A0f7aC73909010b5435768d3B5462`. Routing is resolved from [the route asset](../assets/analytics/premiumseller-routes.json), not a second address registry. [The read-only interface](../assets/analytics/premiumseller-interface.json) records the reviewed explorer-returned runtime fingerprint and source ID `netnet-premium-seller-implementation-20261010`. A fresh exact seller runtime match, immutable dependency getters, dependency code presence, and NET9/USDG6 decimal reads are required at the pinned block. The explorer's source-verification assertion is not an independent compilation. A current seller runtime match does **not** audit historical implementations or dependency behavior; in particular, code presence and decimals do not prove an upgradeable USDG implementation's historical semantics.

Default history starts at the canonical deployment receipt, freshly checked for successful creation of the seller and agreement with its block header. A `--since-days` request finds the first block whose timestamp is at or after the requested cutoff, including every block sharing the cutoff second and never silently including an earlier second. The range ends at the pinned block. Lifetime totals are only exposed when the requested range includes creation and the relevant collection completed with a confirmed final snapshot recheck; otherwise observed subtotals remain separate. A complete narrow-window answer is not a lifetime answer.

For a manual read-only scan, the existing [creation-boundary evidence](premium-seller-contracts.md#creation-boundary-not-transaction-history) identifies block **11,439,688**, transaction `0xbc4e2476b101f79753191364a4bc83fe6ff053c49998c48674c97521149f3c3e`, at **2026-07-16 17:32:50 UTC**. These are October 10 explorer-correlated deployment metadata, not a newly RPC-verified receipt or financial history. Before using that inclusive lower bound for a lifetime claim, fetch the successful creation receipt and canonical header, verify the created seller address and block/hash agreement, then scan through the pinned endpoint with disclosed complete coverage. The first observed sale is not deployment; if creation cannot be validated, retain observed interval subtotals and leave lifetime totals unavailable.

Scans are newest-first, at most 100,000 blocks per initial request with existing bounded splitting. The command has a bounded 4,000-member request allowance so full historical traversal is not structurally excluded by the smaller basic-command budget. Walltime, response size, event rows, receipt caps and recovery limits still apply. A large lifetime scan can stop partially: retain its explicitly labeled **observed subtotal**, requested/covered/missing intervals, and stopping reason. Never promote a subtotal to a total. Proven zeros require complete served history plus successful consistency checks; the log provider may still silently omit evidence, an explicit assumption in every scan's coverage.

## Correct emitters and empty-log interpretation

`PremiumSold(uint256 netSold,uint256 usdgSweptRaw)` is emitted by the **seller**, with topic0 `0x79374ec49ec36f76193b30b6763852a2777fce5bc71823cfd79cd3ba7e66763e`; both quantities are nonindexed ABI data words. ERC20 `Transfer(address,address,uint256)` is emitted by the **token contract** (NET or USDG), with indexed sender/recipient and a nonindexed amount. A seller-address query for ERC20 Transfer logs can legitimately be empty because its emitter filter is wrong. Native ETH movements are not ERC20 Transfer events and need different evidence.

An empty `eth_getLogs` response is never proof that a contract is inactive, unused or dead. Check the chain, canonical address, ABI topic/layout, emitter and requested range, and report exact served coverage and provider-completeness assumptions. The collector does not infer lifecycle status from empty logs or call `active()` to decide historical activity. Even a true execution-eligibility flag can coexist with no executions, and a false flag says nothing about prior sales.

The fresh pinned `lastExecuteAt()` read cross-checks completed scans. If it records an execution within the requested range, the latest observed PremiumSold must have a block timestamp matching that state. Empty history **or nonempty history omitting the latest sale** is otherwise inconsistent: totals remain unavailable, while observed event subtotals survive. `lastExecuteAt = 0` plus a fully served empty range can support zero reported sales under the provider assumption, never an “inactive” classification. A nonzero last execution before a narrow since-days window permits zero matching sales in that window, not a lifetime zero.

## Separate quantities and reconciliation

- `metrics.event_reported` contains count, gross minted NET sold, event USDG proceeds, exact volume-weighted realized price and realized-price range. It distinguishes `observed_subtotal`, `requested_window_total` and `lifetime_total`.
- `metrics.receipt_confirmed` independently reports the gross USDG Transfer amounts matched from seller to canonical Treasury for those sale events. It is not an independent census of every Treasury inflow, a Treasury net balance change, or fiat USD.
- Full output retains per-sale block/transaction/log identity, exact realized price, remittance candidates and status, optional NET-to-pair transfer evidence, and separate pair-to-seller USDG output corroboration. Summary retains the aggregates and coverage rather than the transaction inventory.

Sales deduplicate by canonical transaction hash and log index, with conflicting identity rejected. Every observed sale is bound to its successful Context-validated receipt, including exact emitter, transaction, block/hash and event data. Seller-to-Treasury USDG transfers are partitioned by preceding PremiumSold boundaries, not summed transaction-wide and reused for every sale. A unique preceding remittance with exactly the event proceeds can be matched once. Missing, mismatched or ambiguous segments, unmatched seller-to-Treasury transfers and receipt events omitted from the log scan remain explicit; they prevent complete receipt totals. Multiple PremiumSold events in one transaction violate the reviewed one-hour interval (the timestamp is written before external calls): observations and distinct candidate segments remain visible, but no complete totals or receipt-confirmed sale amounts are inferred from that impossible history.

A receipt sample is not lifetime reconciliation: three matching receipts establish only those three sales, even if a transfer index lists many more NET and USDG legs. Full receipt-confirmed lifetime claims require validated creation-to-end discovery and receipt-local matching for every included sale, plus disclosed index/log coverage. Index-derived proceeds and VWAP otherwise remain separately labeled observed subtotals with unverified receipt coverage; rounded index values cannot establish raw-unit equality. The [supplemental transfer-index procedure](protocol-revenue-analytics.md#shared-period-ledger) already covers authorized `alchemy_getAssetTransfers` discovery and its exclusions.

The reviewed implementation snapshots seller USDG immediately before the swap and transfers **only the post-swap increase** to Treasury. Pre-existing donated seller cash is not swept despite the event field name `usdgSweptRaw`. Unrelated donations, tax-collector remittances and other Treasury flows are excluded by exact token/sender/recipient filters. NET mint and NET seller-to-pair transfer evidence is shown separately when unambiguous: gross minted NET need not equal the pool's actual input after transfer tax. Neither present-day tax settings nor a fixed tax percentage is applied retrospectively.

Realized execution price is `USDG6 proceeds / NET9 gross input`, converted to whole USDG per NET. The aggregate price is **sum(proceeds) / sum(gross NET)**, not the mean of individual prices or today's market mark. Exact rational numerator/denominator pairs avoid rounding and floating-point money. Zero gross input has no defined realized price. Pair output transfer evidence is not an independent pair-balance/invariant audit.

The strict TWAP-above-two-times-backing gate and rounded 99%-of-TWAP minimum do not guarantee the realized sale price exceeds twice backing, and do not imply a price above two USDG. These reports do not assume USDG's fiat parity, transaction costs, profit, recognized revenue, tax disposition, Treasury net balance change, or a one-for-one RFV delta. Consult the source-evidence and contract references for execution mechanics, not these historical sums as an execution recommendation.
