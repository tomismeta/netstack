# Fresh Credit accounting

Use the read-only `credit` workflow for nnUSDG claims, current lending liquidity, accrued debt and pending fee-share entitlement. Contract semantics and exact evidence limits: [Credit contracts](credit-contracts.md). No packaged financial observations, transaction history, account positions or balances are used. Requests go through the existing bounded Context/canonical RPC; an access denial is terminal, not a reason to switch providers.

```sh
python3 scripts/analytics.py credit --block latest-2 --detail full --json
python3 scripts/analytics.py credit --account 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000001 --block latest-2 --detail summary --json
```

`--account` is an optional exact20-byte address, not a wallet connection. `--max-markets` defaults32; `--max-adapters` defaults16. Collection is bounded by the existing750 RPC-member budget and deadline. Increase bounds only for a deliberately broader bounded snapshot; hitting them leaves unknown totals rather than truncating silently. `--output` may checkpoint **outside** the installed package. Do not turn checkpoints into durable packaged financial history.

## What is answered

- **What backs nnUSDG, and what is my claim?** Pinned accrued vault assets, stored shares, pending fee dilution, idle USDG, adapters and market claims. Optional `account` reports actual share balance and `convertToAssets`, plus gates. The output retains observed values before final snapshot confirmation; `confirmed_vault_accounting` is populated only after required discovery/read coverage and recheck succeed.
- **Can I withdraw?** Idle liquidity, current liquidity adapter/data, gates and per-market available loan assets are separate from depositor asset claims. `maxWithdraw=0` is the source's unconditional convention, not an answer that funds cannot be withdrawn. No state-only output guarantees a future successful transaction, slippage or allowance-dependent action.
- **How much is borrowed / what is the account's leverage?** Discover the union of vault adapter markets, current router listings and immutable Loopback market; include chain4663 and singleton+marketID on every row. Read six stored market fields, matched adaptive IRM rate, calculate exactly accrued debt/supply/fee dilution, then optional account collateral/debt/health at reported oracle. No claim of discovery of every Morpho market or every borrower. Unknown oracle semantics are labelled even for known deployment hashes; new variants are explicit unsupported records.
- **What has the fee recipient earned?** Pending vault performance/management fee shares and their current dilution-adjusted asset equivalents, plus pending market fee shares/assets and singleton recipient. For a selected Morpho fee recipient, the accrued supply claim includes its pending fee shares; stored shares remain separately reported. These are current entitlements. They are **not lifetime earnings, realized cash receipts, Treasury ownership or immediately withdrawable amounts**. `cash_fee_receipts` and `lifetime_earnings` remain null, not0.

No event scan is needed to enumerate this current indexed scope. An empty log response is therefore never substituted for state. This method can show nonzero assets and debt even when a provider serves empty logs. Router labels such as pending or launched are not used as state evidence.

## Units, reconciliation and unknowns

All raw asset quantities in supported markets are USDG6; shares and collateral retain their own raw units. USDG raw units are not integer dollars. Ratios use exact numerator/denominator strings, never binary floating point. Vault shares default to source-defined18 decimals but live decimals and virtual shares are read. Morpho virtual offsets (1 asset/1e6 shares) differ from vault offsets. `stored_market` remains distinct from `accrued_market`; available liquidity is accrued supply minus accrued debt, not the singleton's pooled token balance. Multiple market caps share IDs and cannot be added together.

Unsupported adapters/IRMs/assets, bounded discovery, runtime mismatches, failed dependency bindings, oracle reverts required for selected-account health and snapshot recheck failure prevent complete selected-scope output. A reverted oracle preserves known debt and leaves health/mark unknown; it is not a zero collateral-price fallback. Permission/integrity errors are never swallowed. Optional global/lifetime coverage remains separately unproven even when the selected snapshot completes.

Do not sum vault assets + adapter claims + market assets: these are overlapping accounting layers. Do not sum depositor claims into protocol-owned reserves. RFV remains responsible for Sleeve beneficial exposure. Its explicit Reports stored-debt convention is preserved; this workflow's economically accrued debt is not a silent change to that publisher figure. Historical earnings cannot be derived from current assets minus deposits without full cash flows, shares/ownership changes, liabilities, fees, losses and distribution history. Core POL reconstruction remains unsupported; the18432.08USDG discrepancy is unchanged.

## Routing “Sleeve leverage”

“Sleeve leverage” is not one contract getter or the leverage of a typical Credit user. Use [reserve/Sleeve accounting](reserve-analytics.md#plain-language-rfv-requests) for the beneficially attributable portfolio, then the following read-only procedure for debt. The [RWA strategy](rwa-strategy.md#bought-inventory-and-credit-funded-recycling) explains financing, not current balances.

1. **Pin identity, generation and perimeter.** Pin chain4663, block `B`, hash and timestamp. Resolve the Sleeve custody address from RFV's `metrics.sleeve.owner` and the [managerSleeve registry](../assets/addresses/contracts/manager-sleeve-and-morpho-singleton-infrastructure.json), currently `0x498752D5fa0600CBd613074C151Abe15B3FeC7CB`. This is the Safe custody account, **not** the `sleeve.json` SleeveBondDesk at `0x732b…15B3`, a Credit router or a Morpho singleton. For Safe implementation-dependent attribution, read `masterCopy()` at `B` and bind its runtime; a deployment constructor or unpinned explorer implementation is not the current implementation proof. Retain predecessor markets/custodians with exposure; do not combine generations just because their labels match.
2. **Collect the supported current scope at the same block.** Run `python3 scripts/analytics.py rfv --scope net-assets --block B --deadline 600 --detail full --json`, then `python3 scripts/analytics.py credit --account 0x498752D5fa0600CBd613074C151Abe15B3FeC7CB --block B --detail full --json` (replace `B` with the pinned numeric block). Credit discovers `vault.adaptersLength()` → `adapters(i)` → supported adapter `marketIdsLength()` → `marketIds(i)`, union `CreditRouter.listedMarkets()` and `Loopback.marketId()`. Its [interface](../assets/analytics/credit-interface.json) supplies the exact getters; [routes](../assets/analytics/credit-routes.json) bind addresses. Read `Morpho.idToMarketParams(id)` and verify the parameter hash for every `(chain,singleton,id)`. RFV instead seeds catalog markets plus live Loopback and a bounded recent direct-Sleeve `Supply`/`SupplyCollateral` search. Neither collector enumerates every beneficial borrower.
3. **Trace the position owner, not the cash receiver.** In the reviewed [Loopback source](credit-contracts.md#loopback-economics-and-oracle-route), `turbo` supplies collateral for its immediate `msg.sender`; its callback calls `Morpho.borrow(params,assets,0,account,address(this))`. Debt belongs to `account`, not Loopback, even though Loopback receives the USDG. A normal Sleeve Safe call to Loopback therefore leaves the position under the Sleeve Safe; Loopback does **not** automatically hide it under another address. An intermediary calling Loopback may instead be the position owner: require its actual call path and evidenced Sleeve claim before attribution. `isAuthorized(account,operator)` proves permission only, not beneficial ownership. The vault's MorphoMarketV1AdapterV2 supplies under **its own address** for vault depositors; its `supplyShares(id)` / `expectedSupplyAssets(id)` are lender claims, not Sleeve borrower debt. Current CreditRouter implementation source remains unavailable; do not invent its borrower-routing semantics.
4. **Expand only on evidence, and record disposition.** Public Sleeve execution/transfer history, Loopback `Turbo(account,…)` / `Unwind(account,…)`, and Morpho position events provide candidates. For each candidate record `(B,chain,singleton,id,positionOwner,beneficialOwner,attributionEvidence,disposition)`; use `included`, `excluded` or `ownership_unresolved`. Inspect receipt-local caller/onBehalf/receiver and applicable intermediary source/claim getters. A shared deployer, a transfer, Safe signer or entire product balance is insufficient. For supplemental Morpho history obtain the imported `src/libraries/EventsLib.sol` from the [verified source](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/0x9D53d5E3bd5E8d4Cbfa6DB1ca238AEA02E651010): the contract catalog's empty `events` array is not proof that Morpho emits none. `Borrow(bytes32,address,address,address,uint256,uint256)` indexes `id,onBehalf,receiver` (topics1/2/3); `SupplyCollateral(bytes32,address,address,uint256)` indexes `id,caller,onBehalf`. Do not apply the same owner-topic filter to both. Retain searched ranges, deployment boundaries, receipt verification and unsearched intervals; receipt-confirmed candidates alone do not prove discovery completeness.
5. **Read and accrue each unique position.** Read `position(id,positionOwner)` → `(supplyShares,borrowShares,collateral)` and `market(id)` → `(totalSupplyAssets,totalSupplyShares,totalBorrowAssets,totalBorrowShares,lastUpdate,fee)`. For an evidenced owner in the collector's supported market union, rerun `credit --account OWNER --block B`; other markets need explicit supplemental reads, not silently truncated totals. Use the matched IRM's `borrowRateView(params,storedMarket)` and exact ordered integer accrual at `B`'s timestamp. Debt is `ceil(borrowShares*(accruedBorrowAssets+1)/(totalBorrowShares+1000000))`, in USDG6 for supported markets. Do not treat shares as USDG or collateral units as18 by default. Read token decimals. For health, `floor(collateral*oracle.price()/1e36)` is raw loan-asset value, with LLTV WAD; it is not an independently validated portfolio mark. Report mark source, timestamp/age and freshness limits; an oracle revert leaves known debt intact and health unknown.
6. **Reconcile and answer.** Deduplicate by `(chain,singleton,id,positionOwner)` before summing attributable debt. Reconcile a historical debt-share bridge as opening shares + `Borrow.shares` − `Repay.shares` − `Liquidate.repaidShares` − `Liquidate.badDebtShares` = closing shares; interest changes asset debt without minting borrower shares. Use event definitions from the same imported source. Reconcile collateral separately, and keep the collector's adapter `sum(expectedSupplyAssets)==realAssets` and vault idle/adapter/growth-cap checks; these validate lending assets, not beneficial borrower completeness. Require matching final block-hash rechecks across collections. Report direct and additionally attributed debt separately, unresolved owners/markets/marks and whether the requested perimeter is complete. A zero `position(id,Sleeve).borrowShares` proves zero debt only for that key at `B`, not every market, predecessor or evidenced indirect claim. Nor is it evidence that indirect debt must exist. No historical debt amount is an expected result.

State the ratio explicitly: **gross assets / net equity** uses `A / (A − L)`, while **debt / net equity** uses `D / (A − L)`, where `A` is attributable assets counted once, `L` all included liabilities and `D` the interest-bearing debt subset. Use one pinned block, a common evidenced valuation unit, and disclose the ownership/asset/liability perimeter. These are analyst-defined balance-sheet ratios, not publisher “True RFV,” liquidation thresholds or guaranteed unwind proceeds. Missing marks, ownership or liabilities leave a complete ratio unavailable; nonpositive equity leaves these leverage multiples undefined. A known-scope ratio must be labelled as such, not “all Sleeve leverage.”

For a specific borrowing position, report collateral value, accrued debt and the market's evidenced LTV/health separately. Collateral/debt coverage is not gross-assets/equity leverage. Preserve Reports' stored-debt convention only when reproducing that publisher memo; do not substitute it for economically accrued debt in a separately labelled leverage analysis.

Count a vault share claim **or** its attributable adapter/market look-through, never both. Posted collateral is no longer a wallet balance; count it once, then subtract its debt once. Borrow proceeds already reinvested into another included asset are not an additional cash asset. Market-wide borrowing is owed by all borrowers, not automatically by the Sleeve; the vault's loan receivable is not a liability of its depositors. Eliminate internal receivable/payable pairs only when both sides are evidenced inside the declared consolidated perimeter. Keep own-NET exposure and publisher-only Team Safe holdings distinct from external economic assets. These procedures do not clear Core POL reconstruction or `pol_reconstruction_supported=false`.

## Fresh historical fee/receipt investigation recipe

The CLI intentionally does not advertise automatic lifetime fee or earnings reconstruction. A supported historical answer requires all of the following, collected fresh for a user-selected interval and never persisted into package assets:

1. Pin chain/block/hash/time and establish implementation/deployment boundaries. Enumerate adapter additions/removals, market creation and router list/delist events, not just today's lists. Confirm counters/state where available and retain old removed markets with exposure. Empty `eth_getLogs` must be checked against deployment and nonzero accrued/issued-share state; it cannot prove no fees or no activity.
2. Vault: obtain `AccrueInterest`, `Deposit`, `Withdraw`, share `Transfer`, `SetPerformanceFee`, `SetManagementFee`, `SetPerformanceFeeRecipient`, `SetManagementFeeRecipient`, `SetReceiveSharesGate`, and relevant gate/role/adapter configuration changes. Resolve recipient identity **at the event**, not from today's recipient. A zero-address share mint is evidence of issued shares; associate it with the receipt-local accrual, separating deposit mints and fees. Reconcile opening/closing share supply and recipient holdings, including transfers, redemptions and changes in ownership.
3. Morpho: obtain `CreateMarket`, `AccrueInterest`, `SetFee`, global `SetFeeRecipient`, `Supply`, `Withdraw`, `Borrow`, `Repay`, `Liquidate`, collateral changes and authorization-independent transfers where relevant. Fee supply shares are internal singleton positions, not ERC20 `Transfer` events. Reconcile opening/closing market and recipient supply shares; distinguish repayments of principal, interest accrual, bad-debt losses and fee entitlement.
4. Verify successful transaction receipts and identities for candidate real cash receipts. Require an actual USDG transfer to the relevant recipient for a cash claim. Vault fee-share mint does not itself create USDG receipts; a later withdrawal may redeem principal, transferred shares and fees together, so provenance/allocation cannot be inferred from recipient address alone. Adapter `Skim` rewards may be other tokens or unrelated donations. Turbo/Unwind surplus belongs to the borrower unless separate evidence proves otherwise.
5. Publish observed event subtotals, receipt-confirmed amounts, unreconciled intervals and requested-window totals separately. Full totals require complete served discovery, reconciled opening/closing state, receipt coverage and confirmed snapshot. Missing source for current CreditRouter/stock oracles limits semantic attribution; unknown histories stay unknown. Do not declare a lifetime total from a partial or empty scan.
