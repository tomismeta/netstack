### Evidence levels are not interchangeable

1. **Publisher interface:** an ABI literal says how the app expects to call a function. It can omit administrative functions, contain old names, be reused across generations or be wrong.
2. **Dispatcher match:** the explorer runtime contains the selector branch. This supports an interface lead, **not** its name, return layout, caller permissions or economic meaning. Unknown selectors stay unnamed.
3. **Source or targeted runtime analysis:** identified source bodies or exact byte offsets support the described conditions. Explorer source-verification labels remain explorer claims unless independently reproduced. A selected branch is not exhaustive decompilation or an audit.
4. **Pinned state:** chain/block/hash/time, deployed code and relevant configuration/financial reads must agree at one block. Current permissions cannot be inferred from a constructor or historical event.
5. **End-to-end validation:** dependencies, proxy implementations, authority, economic invariants, actual transfers and failure paths require further evidence. No catalog entry claims this level or a security warranty.

The canonical public RPC returned **HTTP403 before a head could be pinned**, separately observed October 7 and October 10. No current financial values, pinned `eth_getCode` or current storage-based permissions are claimed. Explorer source/runtime reads succeeded independently. Source-recovery failures and scope live in [validation](../assets/contracts/validation.json) and the generation-specific references. An unavailable Solidity source is not replaced by an ABI-only “full validation” claim; access failures do not prohibit future independently authorized research.

## Genesis: entitlement is not payment or custody

In the retrieved [explorer listing](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/0x575b7B7c97Ef3E21C82DAeB427899d583e1E913f):

- `purchase(uint256)` spends caller USDG, updates per-account purchased amounts and an append-only registry, and attempts the certificate best-effort. The implementation uses Constants50k/2k/15k caps/minimum; older interface comments300k/10k are not authoritative implementation constants.
- `finalize()` is permissionless once its wired/sale-cap or deadline/minimum conditions hold. It atomically splits proceeds70/30 Treasury/POL, mints NET through Treasury for pool and purchasers, sends LP to Treasury, then enables staking/bonds/tax and checkpoints the oracle. Reverting dependencies revert the transaction; this does not prove finalization actually happened.
- `claim()` is caller-specific. Vested NET is linear over432000 seconds from finalizeTime, capped at the purchased total, less `claimedNet`; a zero claim returns zero. Entitlement, currently claimable amount, successfully paid NET and actual remaining custody are different quantities.
- `wire(address,address)` is deployer-only and once under Wired, not a continuing arbitrary administrator. `refund()` is available only for caller purchases after a failed sale; it zeros `purchasedRaw` but does **not** decrement `raisedRaw` or erase registry history.

For real reconciliation, pin code/state and all dependencies; read the full bounded registry and deduplicate purchasers. Use each account's purchased/claimed/claimable getters and reconcile actual events/transfers, not certificate counts or website totals. Aggregate **per-account** integer entitlement, not individually rounded purchase rows. After established full vest, remaining liability is `sum(purchasedNetOf - claimedNet)`; compare with same-block NET custody. Before full vest, split vested and unvested liabilities. A surplus can reflect donations/rounding and is not automatically profit. No present finalized flag, account count, payout total, balance or solvency result was obtained here.

## Sleeve successor: inventory and remittance

The current app registry assigns `rwaDesk` to **SleeveBondDesk**, indexed September30, and retains V1/V2 for legacy notes. The app's `struct RwaDesk` ABI label does not establish identical implementation across generations. The September28 announcement is not the September30 deployment receipt.

Targeted inspection of [explorer runtime](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/api/v2/smart-contracts/0x732b3D1d3E8912CaE75164FA14a6E1c4C64615B3) supports these **conditional branches**:

- Subscription decreases existing NET inventory and records vesting liability; the inspected path does not mint NET. Its DeskBond event fee field is literal zero.
- Conversion resolves the router dynamically, approves the subscription input, calls supplied route data at that target, measures received RWA, checks minimum/oracle bounds, remits RWA to the fixed Sleeve and measures the recipient's actual increase. Remaining subscription USDG goes to Sleeve, not a Treasury fee; final Desk USDG and RWA residual balances and allowance-reset conditions are checked.
- Inventory deposit is fixed Sleeve-or-manager gated and measured by actual NET balance increase, bounded by inventoryMax; custody checks reserve `inventory + owedNet`. Manager-only configuration gates and caller-note redemption paths are separately identified.
- The inspected `skim(address)` path has no caller authority gate, but sends surplus to the fixed Sleeve and protects NET inventory plus owed notes. Trigger authority is not ownership of the skimmed assets.

This is **not** verified Solidity, an audited arbitrary-route boundary, dependency execution proof or current inventory/menu/halt evidence. The four formerly unexplained selectors now have targeted runtime meanings: protected inventory withdrawal, active-note count, manager-set additional price floor and its getter. Their names/full ABI remain unavailable; matching names in unrelated ABIs cannot fill that gap. Pin all three generations separately for legacy claims, custody and remittance. Launch caps and historical deployment assertions are never current financial inputs.

## Safe use of the catalog

Use function entries to explain user-operated workflows, identify required evidence and design authorized read-only research. Do not generate ready-to-sign/submit artifacts or execute write functions. Read-only helpers under `assets/analytics` deliberately retain their narrower view/pure ABI contracts; this descriptive catalog does not relax them. Missing catalog coverage is a research gap, not a universal prohibition. Follow the [guardrail](guardrail.md), [safety rules](safety.md) and [address-generation guidance](addresses-and-roles.md).
