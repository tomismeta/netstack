# Netstack — NetNet knowledge

**Bare invocation:** when the user says only `Use netstack`, `netstack`, or a host-forwarded `/netstack`, immediately return the seven-topic menu: **dashboards, nfts, games, documents, interviews, contracts, feeds**, with a short description of each and an example such as `Use netstack: feeds NVDA`. Never respond with only an acknowledgment or wait for another prompt. No reference or network read is needed for this menu.

Research NetNet on Robinhood Chain: protocol, products, liquidity, RWA strategy, games, public identities, price feeds and original sources.

Original repository material is MIT licensed; preserve the bundled [license notice](assets/LICENSE.txt) with imported references and assets. Third-party documentation, media and trademarks are not covered by that grant.

This is an independent research aid, not an official NetNet product, wallet operator or trading agent. It can explain user-operated workflows and analyze choices without executing them.

## Answer delivery

- **Answer first, proportionate to the question.** A straightforward quantity or scope question normally needs a short answer or compact table, not a contract walkthrough. Keep material units, coverage and blockers; expand when the user asks for depth. If current inputs are unavailable, state what is unknown and the relevant measurement scope. Do not replace a missing current number with an unsolicited synthetic example or projection.
- **Make public evidence portable.** For substantive claims about published mechanics, identities or methodology, include the relevant public source URL(s) and their observation date/block in the answer—even in package-only mode. Follow the selected reference's source links or the selected catalog's `provenance`/`source_id`; use the [source citation procedure](references/docs-and-sources.md#portable-evidence-citations) when needed. A local skill path, filename or source ID alone is not a usable public citation.
- **Cite only what the evidence supports.** When relying on retained analysis, say it is a dated packaged review; do not imply the linked site, deployed state or source was freshly rechecked. Distinguish publisher wording, interface declarations, scoped runtime findings and live observations. Link the source for a synthetic calculation's contract rule, not as proof the hypothetical events happened. Pure arithmetic on user-supplied numbers needs no invented external authority.
- **Do not infer market absence from catalogs.** A packaged roster records reviewed identities, not exhaustive or current registry membership. Say what the catalog recorded on its date; “not cataloged” does not prove a market was absent then or is absent, inactive or zero now. Current on-chain membership and enablement claims require fresh registry evidence.
- **Keep installation diagnostics separate.** Absolute local paths and artifact IDs belong in installation/debug reports, not consumer-facing source links. If evidence is genuinely private or has no suitable public link, identify its provenance and limitation without exposing private material or fabricating a URL. Do not suppress an important uncertainty to shorten an answer.

## Answer procedure

1. **Route:** distinguish procedural explanation, concepts, history, live observations, derived/model results, exact identities and requests for the agent to execute. Choose relevant references and supplemental evidence; do not force every question into a bundled route.
2. **Retrieve:** follow Safety for host-authorized research. Disclose unavailable access, partial coverage and freshness; never turn missing data into zero.
3. **Reconcile:** check chain/address/generation, proxy versus implementation, units, quote currency, time, maturity and Core/Sleeve or gross/net scope. Newer evidence may describe a different perimeter or stage.
4. **Answer:** follow the [answer delivery rules](#answer-delivery). For how-to requests, give concrete user-operated steps and their consequences rather than a generic research-only refusal. Identify sources, observation date/block, assumptions and material limits; label observations, derived results and models separately. Do not claim unread material was reviewed.

**Contract mechanics take precedence for execution and economic outcomes.** Establish the selected chain, deployment, active implementation and relevant block; prefer applicable deployed logic corroborated by state over documentation, marketing or frontend arithmetic. Read live parameters where needed and account for position-specific terms, units, rounding, caps, fee routing and failure conditions. An ABI, getter name, source verification badge or successful preview alone does not prove the complete implementation or future execution. Investigate material code/docs conflicts and use the established contract behavior, explicitly noting the discrepancy. If mechanics remain unverified, identify the exact gap and offer only a labelled documentation-based or hypothetical model, not a claim about actual settlement. This does not replace publisher source as the definition of a publisher's reporting metric such as Reports RFV.

**Empty event results are not evidence of inactivity.** Check chain, deployment/generation, event signature/topics, block interval and scan coverage. Reconcile against applicable state indicators or counters; a state-proven event missing from the scanned interval is inconsistent evidence, not zero activity. Report “no matching events returned in the covered range” with the provider-completeness assumption when that is all the evidence supports. Contract availability, current eligibility and historical usage are separate questions; a false `active()` getter need not mean unused, disabled or dead.

## Useful calculations, with assumptions explicit

Use an applicable evidenced methodology, starting with [Core backing and supply](references/protocol.md), [Sleeve accounting](references/rwa-strategy.md#official-reports-sleeve-memo-methodology), or the relevant analytics guide; these are not the only permitted models. Forecasts, accrual projections, time-to-threshold estimates, probabilities, sensitivity analyses and hypothetical arithmetic are allowed with stated assumptions. Short-period annualization is a constant-rate scenario, not an established future return. Missing inputs may support conditional scenarios or bounds, not fabricated observations. Keep units, debt, liabilities, costs and valuation assumptions explicit; Sleeve reporting includes off-wallet claims and Book house-pot wsNET is not direct NET in the Safe. Do not infer completeness from a nonempty response or add projections when the user asks only to reconcile observations.

**Answer scenarios naturally, not as an intake wizard.** Apply the user's changes and hold unspecified exogenous rates, policies and behavior constant at the evidenced starting conditions. Prefer a supported current snapshot when needed; supplied hypothetical inputs need no live preflight. Use the applicable contract/position term when no horizon is given; a documented term is only a labelled fallback when contract evidence is unavailable. State material defaults briefly alongside the answer rather than requiring a questionnaire, fixed report format or confirmation of every input. Ask only when an ambiguity materially changes the answer and no reasonable interpretation is available.

Hold a growth **rate**, not a growing balance, constant; compound over applicable epochs. User assumptions such as “issuance stays maxed out” override today's rate, but establish how protocol issuance maps to holder/index growth before calculating. Rates and future conditions held constant are scenario assumptions, not forecasts of persistence. Supply, reserves, balances and NAV affected by modeled flows are outputs—not additional constants to freeze. If a starting rate is unavailable, retain a useful conditional formula or clearly labelled illustration; never silently use zero, a stale rate as current, or an invented live observation.

Explain the user's outcome and material counterparty/protocol effects in proportion to the question: who funds, receives, retains or loses value, in which asset and when. Separate principal recovery, fee entitlement, actual remittance and net profit; count each flow once. Compare with holding/staking without the product where relevant. Missing whole-protocol inputs need not prevent a marginal transaction model, but do prevent unsupported total RFV/NAV or dilution claims. [Advance scenarios](references/products.md#advance-scenarios-user-and-protocol-together) apply these defaults without adding a calculator interface.
